# Regex patterns (Python syntax) for strings reproduced VERBATIM.
# One per line. Must match the WHOLE string to suppress translation.
# Also used by tr-lint to verify fragments survived unchanged.

# Slovene court case numbers:  I K 12345/2024,  II Kp 987/2023
[IVX]+\s+[A-ZČŠŽ][a-zčšž]{0,3}\s+\d+/\d{4}
# Generic file/reference numbers:  123-45/2024-6
\d+[-/]\d+([-/]\d+)*
# Dates in Slovene/German format:  5. 3. 2024   05.03.2024
\d{1,2}\.\s?\d{1,2}\.\s?\d{2,4}\.?
# Monetary amounts with currency
[\d.,]+\s?(EUR|USD|CHF|€|\$)
# Statute short forms:  KZ-1, ZKP, ZPP, ZUP
[A-ZČŠŽ]{2,6}(-\d+[A-Z]?)?
# Bare identifiers / codes
[A-Z0-9]{2,}([-/][A-Z0-9]+)+
# Email and URLs
[\w.+-]+@[\w.-]+\.\w+
https?://\S+
